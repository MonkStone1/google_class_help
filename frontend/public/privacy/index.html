Privacy Policy

Service: Classroom Dashboard (https://monkstonecor.pp.ua/)

Effective date: 2026-09-23

What this service is

Classroom Dashboard is a personal dashboard for Google Classroom. It is served from this website and reads your Classroom data to show it to you in one place. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or developed by Google.

Google account access (read-only)

When you sign in with Google, the app requests only these read-only permissions:

  • See your courses, course work and your own submissions
  • See course work and submissions for courses you teach
  • See the roster of courses you teach

The app never creates, edits or deletes anything in Google Classroom, and it never posts on your behalf. The requested scopes are documented in ADR-0002 of this project.

What is stored and where

  • Your Google profile basics (name and email address) and a record of your sign-in sessions, stored in the application database on the server that runs this site.
  • Your Google OAuth tokens (access/refresh), stored encrypted at rest in the same database. They are never shown in the interface, never put in browser storage and never logged.
  • A cache of the Classroom content described above (courses, assignments, grades, roster), scoped to your account.
  • Your dashboard preferences (theme, filters, language) in the localStorage of your browser — they never leave your device.

Cookies

The site uses one session cookie (gch_session) to keep you signed in. It is HttpOnly (not readable by scripts), carries the Secure and SameSite attributes, expires automatically (14 days) and is deleted when you sign out. No advertising or tracking cookies are used.

Sharing

Your data is not sold and is not shared with third parties. It is only read by the application itself in order to display it back to you. Logs of the service do not contain your tokens or Google authorization codes.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while your account exists. You can clear the application's Classroom cache at any time from Settings, revoke the app's access in your Google Account settings (which stops all access immediately), or sign out to end your session. To have your account and server-side data deleted entirely, contact the addresses below.

Security

The service is served over HTTPS. Sessions are opaque server-side tokens; Google tokens are encrypted at rest. If you believe you have found a security issue, please report it instead of exploiting it.

Changes

This policy may be updated; the effective date above shows the current version. Continued use after a change means acceptance of the new version.

Contact
